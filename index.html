Architecture

Events become curves; curves become a delta

A day planner turns latent skill into events. Each event becomes a factor curve — gamma for carbohydrate, Bateman for basal insulin. The metabolic core sums them every five minutes.

glucose_in  = carbs + hepatic_output − exercise
glucose_out = insulin × ICR / sensitivity
delta_BG    = α × (glucose_in − glucose_out)

Hepatic output is insulin-suppressed through a Hill function and drains a finite glycogen store. Renal clearance, counter-regulation and a glucagon dump guard the tails; a stochastic glucose-effectiveness term supplies mean reversion inside the band.
